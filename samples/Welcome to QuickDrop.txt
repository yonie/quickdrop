Welcome to QuickDrop!

QuickDrop is a shared drop zone for your home network. Put a file in from
one device and pick it up on another: your PC, your laptop, your phone.

Getting started
  - Drag files or folders onto the window. They land where you drop them.
  - On a phone or tablet, tap Upload.
  - Double-click a file to open it. Right-click for more, like Download.
  - Move icons anywhere you like, and make folders to keep things tidy.
  - Deleted something by accident? Open the Recycle Bin, or press Ctrl+Z.

Try the samples
  - Sample photo.jpg      double-click to view it, or right-click > Edit in Paint
  - Sample document.pdf   double-click to read it
  - Sample archive.zip    double-click to unpack it into a folder
  - this file             you're reading it in Notepad, so go ahead and type

Press F1 or click the ? button for help, and Settings to choose which
tools are switched on.

You can delete the samples whenever you like.
